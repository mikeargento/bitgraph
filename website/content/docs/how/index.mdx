---
title: "How it works"
description: "One atomic action, inside measured hardware."
---

Each BitGraph is one of one: a place in line never used before and never used again.

A BitGraph is an attested record made inside an AWS Nitro enclave running a published, reproducible build.

In one atomic operation, the enclave records a fresh hardware-generated nonce at count N and binds your data’s SHA-256 digest at count N+1. The two records are signed into one proof, attested by the hardware and linked to the previous proof.

The result is a self-contained JSON proof that anyone can verify offline. Your content stays with you; only its 32-byte digest reaches BitGraph.
