### Does BitGraph upload my file?

No. Only its SHA-256 is sent. (A client you did not write could send more; this site’s drop boxes and the published packages send only the hash.)

### Can the same file get two proofs?

Yes. Each is its own BitGraph at its own position, with its own nonce.

### What if someone edits the proof?

Any change to a signed field breaks the signature, and the check fails.

### Why not just trust a timestamp?

A time written by the party a check exists to catch is a claim, not evidence. A BitGraph’s place comes from a counter and a value nobody could predict, signed by measured hardware.

### Does BitGraph use a blockchain?

Not to make a BitGraph: the place in line comes from the enclave, and nothing is read from a chain. Once an hour BitGraph publishes a fingerprint of the line on Base, as a public record of its order.

### Does an outside anchor change the BitGraph?

No. The bind is the same two counts. An anchor someone adds afterward, to a timestamp authority, a log or a blockchain, sits outside the proof and is optional.

### Is a tree weaker than one BitGraph per file?

Every file in it shares one position: they were all bound together, at that point in the sequence. To tell the files apart in order, make them separately.

### What does it cost to check?

Nothing. Verification is free and permissionless.
