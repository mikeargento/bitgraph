BitGraph adds attested recording to [products for provenance, content credentials, trusted capture, timestamping and audit trails](/use-cases). You keep your product, your customers and your brand. Your system submits a digest and receives a proof that travels with your records and can be verified offline.

BitGraph establishes a verifiable position in an enclave’s sequence. Your product can bind identity, timestamps and other evidence to that position.

Licensing is offered through Argento Computing Inc., the company that develops and operates BitGraph.

### Managed recording: Call the BitGraph service.

Your system sends SHA-256 digests; the enclave binds them and returns proofs your product stores and shows. There is no BitGraph infrastructure to operate, and your files never leave your systems.

- Fastest to integrate: one HTTP call, or the SDK
- Collections: one BitGraph for many items, each independently verifiable
- Usage-based terms

### Mechanism license: Run it under your control.

License the recording mechanism to integrate into your own infrastructure and product, with scope, field of use and deployment set by agreement.

- Your product experience, your customer relationship
- White-label where the agreement allows
- BitGraph runs on AWS Nitro today. The mechanism is designed for implementation in other protected environments, with deployment requirements, such as a deployment’s own measured enclave build, agreed as part of the license.

### Strategic: Broader rights.

For organizations interested in the technology and its intellectual property more broadly, including exclusivity in a field or acquisition. Handled confidentially.

- Technical and diligence materials on request, under NDA
- Direct conversation with the inventor

## From first look to agreement.

1.  **Evaluate on your own.** Read the mechanism and the threat model, make real BitGraphs, and verify them offline. No call needed: [the evaluation guide](/integrate/evaluate).
2.  **Fit.** A technical conversation about where the recording step sits in your product and what it should bind.
3.  **Proof of concept.** A working integration against the live service, on your data.
4.  **Agreement.** Scope, deployment model and terms.

No terms are published, and nothing on this site is an offer. The verifier stays available to your customers whatever you choose, so the proofs you issue keep checking.
