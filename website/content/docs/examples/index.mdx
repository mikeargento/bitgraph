title: "Demos"
---

Working examples, each using real BitGraphs.

- [Ask AI](/jev/): two questions made from A’s nonce, answered by an AI, and bound as B.
- [Generate image](/generate/): a picture drawn by code from A’s nonce and bound as B.
- [Sealed exam](/exam/): twenty benchmark questions made from A’s nonce, a model’s answers bound as B, checked offline.
- [Copies](/examples/copies): three files, three places in line: a photograph saved three times, a picture with its video and copy, a record in three versions.
