These three pictures look exactly alike, but they are three separate files. Each file got its own BitGraph, one right after another. You can’t tell them apart by looking. Their proofs can: each one shows whether its file was recorded 1st, 2nd or 3rd.

## A picture, its video, its copy. Three places in line.

A picture made with ChatGPT, a video Grok made from it and a copy Google made of it. Each file got its own BitGraph, one right after another. The proofs show the order the three were recorded in. The makers’ names come from the Content Credentials inside each file, not from the proofs.

## One record, three versions. Three places in line.

A patient’s record saved three times as it changed: a first visit, an allergy added, a dose changed. Each version got its own BitGraph, one right after another. The proofs show the order the three were recorded in. The dates come from the records themselves, not from the proofs, and the patient is invented for this page.
